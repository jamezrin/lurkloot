## 1. Sign in to Twitch or Kick

Open [twitch.tv](https://www.twitch.tv) or [kick.com](https://kick.com) in the same browser and sign in as usual. Lurkloot uses that session. It never asks you for your username or password.

If you want to farm both platforms, sign in to both.

## 2. Open the popup

Click the Lurkloot icon in the toolbar. The first time, a short tour offers to show you around. It takes under a minute, and you can use the arrow keys to move through it. **Skip** is fine too: everything it shows is also in this guide.

## 3. Switch on a platform

Pick **Twitch** or **Kick** in the popup's sidebar, then turn on the switch in the status strip. That strip always shows what is being farmed right now and on which channel.

Each platform has its own switch, so you can farm one or both. Each also has its own queue, games and watchlist.

## 4. Leave it running

That is all. Lurkloot looks at which drops you can still earn, picks a live channel that counts toward one, and claims each reward as soon as it is ready. When a stream goes offline or a reward finishes, it moves on by itself.

Keep the browser open. You can close the popup, use other tabs and do other things. Farming carries on in the background.

## Will I see a stream playing?

Usually not. Most of the time Lurkloot watches without opening a stream, so there is no video and no ads. Sometimes a stream only counts if it is actually playing. Then Lurkloot opens a muted tab for that channel. Leave that tab alone and it closes when it is no longer needed. Because it is a real stream, a Twitch or Kick ad can play in it.

## When there are no drops

If nothing is left to earn, Lurkloot can watch channels from your **Watchlist** instead, so you keep collecting channel points on Twitch, and daily rewards and your watch streak on Kick. Add channels in the **Watchlist** view.

## See what happened

Open **Activity** to see every reward claimed, every channel switch and every finished campaign, including what happened while you were away.
