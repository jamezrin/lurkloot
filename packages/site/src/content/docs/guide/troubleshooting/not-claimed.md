## Is auto-claim on?

**Auto-claim drops** is on by default. If it is off, rewards wait for you to claim them on the platform's drops page.

## Is the platform holding it back?

- **A linked game account.** Many rewards can only be claimed once your game account is connected to Twitch or Kick. The campaign shows **Link your account**. Link it, and Lurkloot claims it on its next check, if the campaign has not ended.
- **A subscription.** A reward that needs one is not released until Twitch counts it. See [Subscription rewards](/guide/farming/subscription-rewards).
- **Not released yet.** Sometimes the platform takes a little while to make a finished reward claimable. Lurkloot keeps checking.

## Was it claimed somewhere else?

A reward you claim yourself, on the platform's site or in another browser, shows as claimed on the next refresh. Check **Activity** and the platform's inventory page.

## Still not claimed?

Copy the log from **Activity** and [report it](/guide/troubleshooting/report-a-problem). Say which campaign and reward it was.
