## Export and import settings

Under **Import / export settings** in Settings:

- **Export settings** saves all your settings to a file: platforms, games, pins, Watchlists, filters and the rest.
- **Import settings** loads a file and replaces your current settings with it.

Use it to back up your setup, or to copy it to another browser or [another browser profile](/guide/accounts/multiple-accounts). The file never contains your Twitch or Kick login.

## Reset Lurkloot

**Reset Lurkloot** erases all of Lurkloot's settings, farming progress and activity history, as if you had just installed it. It stops farming and closes the tabs Lurkloot opened. Your Twitch and Kick accounts stay signed in.

This cannot be undone. Export your settings first if you might want them back.

## Export credentials

**Export credentials** is only for setting up the [CLI](/guide/headless/sign-in). It is a different thing from exporting settings: it saves your Twitch and Kick sign-ins to a file, so that anyone who has the file can act as you on those sites. See [Privacy and account safety](/guide/accounts/privacy).
