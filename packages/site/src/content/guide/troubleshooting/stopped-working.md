Lurkloot shows *Lurkloot has stopped working* when it has stopped itself to avoid doing harm or wasting effort. The toolbar icon also says *not making progress*. There are two reasons:

## No progress despite repeated errors

*Repeated errors and no drop progress for a long time.* Something on Twitch or Kick probably changed, and this version of Lurkloot can no longer earn drops there.

1. Check for an update. Chrome updates extensions on its own, but you can force it from your extensions page: turn on **Developer mode**, then click **Update**.
2. Check the [open issues](https://github.com/jamezrin/lurkloot/issues). Others may already have reported it.
3. If no one has, click **Copy logs & open issue**. It copies the log and opens the bug report form.

## A tab kept reopening

*A tab kept reopening over and over. Reopening has been stopped so you can use your browser normally, and farming is paused for this platform.* See [Tabs keep opening](/guide/troubleshooting/tabs).

## Trying again

**Dismiss and try again** clears the message and starts farming again. If the cause is still there, you will see the message again after a while.
