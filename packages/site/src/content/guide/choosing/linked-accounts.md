Many drop campaigns only give you the reward when your account for that game (Steam, Epic, Riot and so on) is connected to your Twitch or Kick account. You link it once, on the game's or the platform's site. Lurkloot cannot do it for you.

## Spotting them

A campaign that needs linking shows **Link your account** on its card, with a link to where you can do it, and *Account not linked* as its rank reason.

## Choosing what happens to them

Under **Campaigns without a linked account**, in [Rewards to farm](/guide/choosing/rewards-to-farm) or in Settings:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Include** (default) | Farmed like the rest. Link the account to claim the rewards. |
| **Skip** | Never farmed, even when pinned. |
| **Farm last** | Farmed only when no linked campaign can be. They gather in the **Unlinked · farmed last** group. Pinned campaigns and favourite games keep their place. |

**Farm last** is a good middle ground: you still build up progress on campaigns you might link later, without taking time from the ones you can claim now.

## Things to know

- Whether watch time before linking counts depends on the campaign. Check its page on Twitch or Kick if it matters, or link first to be safe.
- If you link an account while Lurkloot is running, it notices on its next refresh.
