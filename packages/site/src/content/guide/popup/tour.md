Click the Lurkloot icon in your browser's toolbar to open the popup. It has three parts: a sidebar, the status strip, and the view you have open.

## The sidebar

The top of the sidebar switches between **Twitch** and **Kick**. Each platform has its own Queue, games, Watchlist and settings, and its own on/off switch. The coloured dot next to each platform shows how it is doing.

Below that, one entry per view:

| View | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Queue** | The campaigns Lurkloot will farm, in the order it will farm them. See [Read the Queue](/guide/popup/queue). |
| **Completed** | Campaigns that are finished (every reward claimed) or expired (ended before they were done). |
| **Games** | Choose which games are farmed, star favourites, and block games. See [Games and categories](/guide/choosing/games). |
| **Watchlist** | Channels to watch when there are no drops. See [Watchlist](/guide/farming/watchlist). |
| **Extensions** | Twitch only. NoPixel and Fortnite rewards. See [NoPixel](/guide/farming/nopixel) and [Fortnite](/guide/farming/fortnite). |
| **Activity** | Everything Lurkloot did: claims, channel switches, finished campaigns. See [Activity and notifications](/guide/popup/activity). |
| **Settings** | Everything else. Use the search box at the top to find a setting by name. |

## The status strip

The strip across the top is always visible. It shows what is being farmed right now, on which channel, and the switch that turns farming on or off for this platform.

On Kick, it also shows a small badge with your Kick viewer level and daily watch streak. See [Kick daily challenges and level](/guide/farming/kick-challenges). The badge is hidden when the popup is too narrow to fit it.

## The toolbar icon

The Lurkloot icon in your toolbar changes to show how things are going. Hover over it for a short description.

| Tooltip says | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| *Farming is off* | No platform is switched on. |
| *Twitch: farming* | Lurkloot is watching something that counts. |
| *Twitch: nothing to farm right now* | It is on, but nothing you can earn is live. It will keep checking. |
| *Twitch: paused* | Farming is paused, for example because you are watching a stream yourself. |
| *Twitch: signed out* | Sign in to the platform in this browser. |
| *Twitch: access blocked* | The platform refused this browser. See [Sign-in and access problems](/guide/troubleshooting/sign-in). |
| *Twitch: not making progress* | Something has gone wrong for a long time. See [Lurkloot has stopped working](/guide/troubleshooting/stopped-working). |

When more than one platform is on, the icon shows the one that needs your attention most.

## The panel on Twitch and Kick

On twitch.tv and kick.com, Lurkloot adds an **Open Lurkloot** button to the page. It opens the same popup in a panel beside the stream. On a channel page, the panel also lets you add that channel to your Watchlist, or remove it.

If you do not want the button, turn off **Show panel on Twitch and Kick** in Settings.

## Tips

The Queue shows short tips about features you might have missed. Turn them off with **Hide tips**.

## Language

The popup is available in 17 languages and follows your browser's language. To choose one yourself, open Settings and change **Language**.
