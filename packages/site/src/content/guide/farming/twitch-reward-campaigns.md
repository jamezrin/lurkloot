Besides game drops, Twitch runs sitewide reward campaigns, such as badge events. They are not tied to one game's streams: watching any channel in the campaign's categories counts. Lurkloot puts them in the same [Queue](/guide/popup/queue) as drops and farms them the same way.

## Rewards that repeat

Some of these campaigns pay out once per day, or once per period. Lurkloot claims today's reward, then waits until the next one opens before farming the campaign again.

## Lanes

Some badge campaigns have more than one *lane*: several badges you can evolve, of which you earn one at a time. Twitch only gives you progress on the lane you have equipped.

When a campaign needs a choice, it shows under **Action required** with a picker, **Pick a lane to earn**. Choose one, and Lurkloot equips it on Twitch and farms it. The lane you are on is marked **Equipped**.

A lane only evolves a badge you already own. If you do not have that badge, watching earns nothing on that lane, so the picker only offers lanes where Twitch reports progress for you.

## Things to know

- A campaign whose progress Twitch never reports is left alone. Lurkloot cannot tell when it is finished, so it does not risk watching it forever.
- These campaigns follow your [Games](/guide/choosing/games) settings like any other. One is only blocked if every category it counts in is blocked.
