When nothing else is left to farm, Lurkloot can watch channels you choose. On Twitch that keeps you earning channel points. On Kick it counts toward your daily rewards, watch streak and viewer level.

## Adding channels

1. Open **Watchlist** in the popup.
2. Type a channel name or paste a channel link, and add it.
3. Drag channels into the order you want.

Each platform has its own list of up to 30 channels. You can also add or remove a channel straight from its page on twitch.tv or kick.com, using the **Open Lurkloot** panel.

## Which channel is watched

The first **live** channel in the list is watched. If it goes offline, Lurkloot moves to the next live one. Put the channels you care about most at the top.

## When the Watchlist is used

By default the Watchlist comes last: it is only used when there are no drops (and, on Twitch, no extension rewards) to farm. Change that in [Watch-source priority](/guide/choosing/watch-source-priority). Move **Watchlist** above **Drops** and Lurkloot watches your channels first, and only farms drops when none of them is live.

## Things to know

- Excluded drop channels do not apply here. A channel you list is always allowed.
- Watchlist channels also help with drops: with **Prefer followed and Watchlist channels** on, a Watchlist channel streaming a drops game is picked first for that campaign.
- On Twitch, you can ask Lurkloot to keep up your [watch streak](/guide/farming/watch-streaks) on a Watchlist channel.
